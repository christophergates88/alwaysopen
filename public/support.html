Support

Help with the Always Open app and service

Reach us: email hello@alwaysopenoffice.com, or open the app and tap Ask us. A person answers, usually the same business day.

Signing in to the app

There is no password. When you sign up, we send you a private link to your business's app. Open that link on your iPhone, or paste it into the app's first screen, and you're in. Lost the link? Email us from the address on your account and we'll send a new one. Anyone you add under Team in the app gets a link of their own.

Notifications

The app buzzes you when a review reply or post is ready for your OK, when Max takes a call, and when a follow-up is due. If they stop, check iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Always Open, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

Approving replies and posts

Nothing goes out in your name until you approve it. Open Approve, read the draft, then tap Approve, Edit, or Skip. You can also swipe a card right to approve or left to skip.

Max, your phone line

Max answers the calls you can't, takes a message, and tells you who called and what they need. Open the Max tab to change your hours, what Max tells callers, and where urgent calls go. Max says on every call that it is not a person and that the call may be recorded.

Your plan and billing

To change your plan, pause, or cancel, tap Ask us in the app or email us. Canceling stops the next charge, and nothing more is billed after that.

Your data

To have your account and its data deleted, email hello@alwaysopenoffice.com from the address on your account and say so. We confirm by email and delete it within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
