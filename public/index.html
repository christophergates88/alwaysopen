How we work

Three rules we don't bend.

No fake reviews

Every review on your page comes from a real customer. We never write them, buy them or trade for them.

Every customer gets asked

We ask everyone after a job, not just the ones who seem happy. It's the fair way, and it's what Google's rules require.

You approve what matters

Anything sensitive, like a reply to an angry customer, comes to your phone first. Nothing goes out in your name that you wouldn't say yourself.

For regulated businesses

Can't share your client list? You don't have to.

Insurance agencies, medical and dental offices, law firms and financial advisors have rules about client information. The Starter plan runs entirely on public information: your Google and Facebook pages, your reviews, and other businesses’ public numbers. We never ask for, receive or store a client’s name or number. We have never needed one.

Nothing private comes in

We work from your public pages. No client names, no policy or account details, no patient or case information.

Replies that say nothing private

A reply never confirms that someone is a client or patient and never mentions details. Anything sensitive comes to you first.

Your rules come first

Every industry has its own rules about advertising and review replies. You tell us what is allowed, and we follow it. If you need sign-off from a parent company, get it before we start.

Compliance Guard

Every word is checked before you see it.

Compliance Guard is a check that runs on every draft for a regulated business before it reaches your phone. It reads the reply or post against your rules and flags anything that shouldn’t go out. A flagged draft can’t be approved until the words change.

How it helps
  • Protects your license and your reputation.
  • Nothing goes out unchecked, and nothing goes out without your OK.
  • A record you can hand to a compliance officer: what was checked, who approved it, and when.
What it checks
  • Promises of an outcome
  • Rates, prices, quotes or discounts
  • Anything that confirms someone is a client or patient
  • Claim or case details
  • Anything that sounds like advice
  • Your own do-not-use list
  • On every plan, for every business: anything offered in return for a review

Questions

Things owners ask first.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. Replies and posts come to your phone in the Always Open app. You tap Approve, or change the words first. Once a month you open a one-page report. That’s the whole job on your side. It takes less time than reading this.

What is the Always Open app?

Your own private app, included with every plan. It shows the replies and posts waiting for your OK, your month in numbers, your monthly records, and a message box that reaches us directly. It opens from a private link, so there's no password to remember, and it's in the App Store for iPhone.

Do you pay for referrals?

No. If a client sends another owner our way, we say thank you and mean it, but there's no payment or discount for it. The same goes for reviews: we never offer anything for one.

What do you need from me to start?

Three things, about ten minutes. Your hours and the best number for urgent calls. One tap in the app to connect Facebook. And a sample reply approved so we sound like you. Your audit, request link, booking link and app work the same day. Invite us onto your Google profile whenever you’re ready: you stay the owner, and you can remove us any time.

Will this get me to number one on Google?

No promises. The honest options are doing nothing, hiring someone, or us. One of those has never moved a ranking. The report shows you which of the other two did.

Do you reply on Yelp?

We write it. You post it. Yelp doesn’t let outside services post for a business, so we hand you the finished reply and a one-tap link. About ten seconds on your end. We would do it for you if Yelp allowed it. They don’t. One more Yelp rule: they ask businesses not to request reviews. So we never ask for Yelp reviews. We’d rather lose a review than get your page flagged. Yelp replies come with the Review alerts beyond Google add-on.

How long until texting works from my business number?

Carriers make every business register before it can text. Think of it as a license for your phone number, with more forms. Approval usually takes one to two weeks. We handle the paperwork and ask you only for a few business details. Until then, nothing waits. Reminders go out by email, and every text is written for you with a one-tap button that sends it from your own phone. Calls, booking, invoices, estimates and your monthly report start the first day.

We can’t share client or patient information. Can we still use you?

Yes. It was built for that. The Starter plan runs on public information only: your reviews, your Google profile and your public numbers. We never receive a client list, a policy, a chart or a case file. We have never needed one. What you get: every review answered in your voice, your profile kept current, and a monthly report on how many people called, asked for directions or visited your site. The rules for your field: Compliance Guard checks every reply and post against them before you see it, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Tell us your rules once and we apply them every time. If you add Max, he takes a caller’s name and number the way a receptionist would. It goes to your phone, not into any client or patient file.

Why pay for this when I could do it myself?

You could. You won’t. Not because you’re lazy. You’re busy, and the work in front of you pays. Meanwhile the review from Tuesday sits there, Thursday’s call went to voicemail, and that customer called the next guy. We do it every day, including the days you’re under a house. About what six hours of office help costs. You approve it. You’re the boss. We’re the ones who remember.

What if it doesn’t bring in new business?

Then stop paying for it. The first month is refundable, there’s no contract, and the monthly report shows calls, direction requests, website clicks and new reviews. Judge it on your own numbers. We do.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, the first month is refundable, and there’s no setup fee. If we’re not worth it, you’ll know by the report, and so will we.

Who am I dealing with?

A real person. Always Open works with businesses anywhere in the U.S., all online and by phone, with software assistants doing the daily work and a person checking it. You can reach us any time at (706) 460-2226 or hello@alwaysopenoffice.com.
