What plumbers ask first

Do I have to sit at a desk for this?

No. Everything comes to your phone in the Always Open app. You tap Approve, or change the words first. Once a month you get a one-page report. You can read it on the tailgate.

What does Max say when someone asks what a job costs?

He shares only the prices you give him, word for word, and never makes one up. Otherwise he takes the details for an estimate and a good time for you to call back.

Will callers know Max isn’t a person?

Yes. He says so at the start of every call, along with the fact that the call may be recorded. If someone wants a person, he takes a message, or puts urgent calls through to your cell if you choose.

Can I keep my business number?

Yes. When you can’t pick up, call forwarding sends the call to Max. We set it up with you, and most phone companies support it.

Do you text my customers?

Only people who reached out to you or agreed to it. A finished-job review request is one text, and only for customers who agreed. On Pro, quote follow-ups and appointment confirmations go out from your business number to customers who said yes, and someone who calls and can’t reach you gets one text back. Texting starts once carriers approve your business number, usually one to two weeks; until then the text is written for you and sends from your own phone.

Will this get me to number one on Google?

No promises. The honest options are doing nothing, hiring someone, or us. One of those has never moved a ranking. The report shows you which of the other two did.

What do you need from me to start?

Three things, about ten minutes. Your hours, service area and the best number for urgent calls. And a sample reply approved so we sound like you. Max’s number is ready the minute you sign up. Your audit, request link, booking link and app work the same day. Invite us onto your Google profile whenever you’re ready: you stay the owner, and you can remove us any time.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s month to month, there’s no setup fee, and the first month is refundable.

Why pay for this when I could do it myself?

You could. You won’t. Not because you’re lazy. You’re busy, and the work in front of you pays.
