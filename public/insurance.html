What agency owners ask first

Will a reply ever confirm that someone is our client?

No. Compliance Guard stops phrases like “your policy” or “your claim” before a reply reaches you, and every reply waits for your approval. If you edit a reply in the app, the edit is checked again.

Do you need our client list or access to our agency system?

No. We work only from public pages: your Google and Facebook profiles, your reviews, and other agencies’ public ratings. We never ask for, receive or store a client’s name or number.

Who approves the wording?

Your compliance contact approves our reply library once. After that, each reply comes to your phone in the Always Open app, and you tap Approve or change the words first. Nothing posts in your agency’s name without your OK.

We’re captive agents. Can we use this?

Yes, with your company’s OK. Get it before we start, and tell us your company’s rules for reviews and social media so we can follow them. If your company gives you a texting system, we never touch it.

What does Max say when someone calls about a claim?

Max takes a message only: their name, number, what they need and a good time to call back. He never discusses policies, coverage, claims, rates or quotes, and he tells every caller up front he isn’t a person and that the call may be recorded. If you choose, urgent calls ring through to your cell.

Does Rate-Shock Radar name other agencies?

Never. We count public reviews about rate increases across the agencies in your town, and you only see the trend and what’s ready for you to approve.

Do you reply in Spanish?

Yes. Spanish reviews get Spanish replies from the same approved library, checked by the same Compliance Guard, and Max speaks Spanish too.

What do you need from us to start?

Manager access to your Google Business Profile, your compliance contact’s OK on the reply library, your license number and any required notices, and any words you never want used. Setup takes about a week.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s month to month, there’s no setup fee, and the first month is refundable.
