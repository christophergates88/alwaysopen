Write a reply. See what Compliance Guard stops.

Compliance Guard is a check that runs on every draft before it reaches your phone. It reads each reply and post against the rules below, and against any words your compliance contact adds, and flags anything that shouldn’t go out. A flagged draft can’t be approved until the words change.

How it helps
  • Protects your license and your agency’s name.
  • Nothing goes out unchecked, and nothing goes out without your tap.
  • A record for your compliance file: what was checked, who approved it, and when.

What it checks:

  • Confirms someone is a client“your policy,” “your claim,” “as a valued client”

  • Talks about rates or pricesdollar amounts, percentages, “lower rates,” quotes, discounts

What agency owners ask first

Will a reply ever confirm that someone is our client?

No. Compliance Guard stops phrases like “your policy” or “your claim” before a reply reaches you, and every reply waits for your approval. If you edit a reply in the app, the edit is checked again.

Do you need our client list or access to our agency system?

No. We work only from public pages: your Google and Facebook profiles, your reviews, and other agencies’ public ratings. We never ask for, receive or store a client’s name or number.

Who approves the wording?

Your compliance contact approves our reply library once. After that, each reply comes to your phone in the Always Open app, and you tap Approve or change the words first. Nothing posts in your agency’s name without your OK.

We’re captive agents. Can we use this?

Yes, with your company’s OK. Get it before we start, and tell us your company’s rules for reviews and social media so we can follow them. If your company gives you a texting system, we never touch it.

What does Max say when someone calls about a claim?

Max takes a message only: their name, number, what they need and a good time to call back. He never discusses policies, coverage, claims, rates or quotes. If you choose, urgent calls ring through to your cell.

Does Rate-Shock Radar name other agencies?

Never. Each Monday we count recent public reviews that mention rate increases across the agencies in your town, and only that count reaches our team; it isn’t kept from week to week. You see what’s ready for you to approve, never another agency’s name. It covers Eatonton, Madison, Greensboro, Milledgeville, Monticello, Gray, Sparta and Macon today.

Do you reply in Spanish?

Yes. Spanish reviews get Spanish replies from the same approved library, checked by the same Compliance Guard, and Max speaks Spanish too.

What do you need from us to start?

Three things, about ten minutes. Your hours and the best number for urgent calls. Your compliance contact’s OK on the reply library, with your license number, any required notices and any words you never want used. And one tap in the app to connect Facebook, if you use it. Your audit and app work the same day. Invite us onto your Google profile whenever you’re ready: you stay the owner, and you can remove us any time.

Will this get me to number one on Google?

No promises. The honest options are doing nothing, hiring someone, or us. One of those has never moved a ranking. The report shows you which of the other two did.

Do you reply on Yelp?

We write it. You post it. Yelp doesn’t let outside services post for a business, so we hand you the finished reply and a one-tap link. About ten seconds on your end. We would do it for you if Yelp allowed it. They don’t. One more Yelp rule: they ask businesses not to request reviews. So we never ask for Yelp reviews. We’d rather lose a review than get your page flagged. Yelp replies come with the Review alerts beyond Google add-on.

Why pay for this when I could do it myself?

You could. You won’t. Not because you’re lazy. You’re busy, and the work in front of you pays. Meanwhile the review from Tuesday sits there, Thursday’s call went to voicemail, and that customer called the next guy. We do it every day, including the days you’re under a house. About what six hours of office help costs. You approve it. You’re the boss. We’re the ones who remember.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s month to month, there’s no setup fee, and the first month is refundable.
