What practice owners ask first

Do I have to sit at a desk for this?

No. Everything comes to your phone in the Always Open app. You tap Approve, or change the words first. Once a month you get a one-page report. You can read it on the tailgate.

Can I keep my business number?

Yes. When you can’t pick up, call forwarding sends the call to Max. We set it up with you, and most phone companies support it.

Do you text my patients?

No. Max takes messages, and you call or text patients back from your own phone. We never text for a practice and never handle patient records.

Will this get me to number one on Google?

No promises. The honest options are doing nothing, hiring someone, or us. One of those has never moved a ranking. The report shows you which of the other two did.

What does Max say when someone asks about fees, treatment or insurance?

He does not get into any of it. He takes a message with the caller’s name, number and what they need, and lets them know the office will call. Compliance Guard checks every reply and post we write for you, and nothing goes out without your OK.

Will callers know Max isn’t a person?

Yes. He says so at the start of every call, along with the fact that the call may be recorded. If someone wants a person, he takes a message, or puts urgent calls through to your cell if you choose.

What do you need from me to start?

Three things, about ten minutes. Your hours, the best number for urgent calls, and your reply wording approved once. Max’s number is ready the minute you sign up. Your audit, request link, booking link and app work the same day. Invite us onto your Google profile whenever you’re ready: you stay the owner, and you can remove us any time.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s month to month, there’s no setup fee, and the first month is refundable.

Why pay for this when I could do it myself?

You could. You won’t. Not because you’re lazy. You’re busy, and the work in front of you pays. Meanwhile the review from Tuesday sits there, Thursday’s call went to voicemail, and that customer called the next guy. We do it every day, including the days you’re under a house.
