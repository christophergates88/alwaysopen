Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Always Open (“we”) is a service of Lake Country Foam Party, LLC, based in Georgia, United States. We help businesses across the U.S. with their Google and Facebook pages, reviews, missed calls, booking and monthly reports. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

  • From you, when you contact us. Your name, email, phone number, business name, town, type of business, the plan you are interested in, any link you share, and anything you write in the notes.
  • From our clients. Access you give us to your Google Business Profile, Facebook page and similar accounts, your business details (hours, services, photos), and the reviews and messages people post publicly about your business.
  • From customers of our clients. When a client uses text features, we handle the phone number and message content of people who call or text that business, and the names and contact details the client gives us for appointment, quote or invoice reminders.
  • From our website. Our website does not use advertising cookies or trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • From the Always Open map. The map at alwaysopenoffice.com/map shows the businesses we work for. To sort them by distance it uses the rough area our hosting provider (Cloudflare) sees for your visit, or, if you tap Near me, the location your browser shares. That location is used for that one request and is not stored. The map is drawn with OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap data, loaded from their servers, which receive your IP address the way any website does. We place businesses on the map using their town or the address they give us, with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder or OpenStreetMap.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and to set up and run the service you ask for.
  • To answer reviews, keep listings current, send texts, and write reports for the business you work with.
  • To send invoices and handle billing.
  • To keep the service safe, fix problems, and meet legal requirements.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

Text messages

For client businesses, we send texts such as replies to missed calls, appointment confirmations and reminders, quote follow-ups, and invoice or payment reminders. We send them only to people who contacted the business or are its customers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. After you reply STOP we may send one last message to confirm.

We do not share mobile phone numbers, or text-messaging consent and opt-in information, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Texting registration

Phone carriers require every business that texts its customers to be registered. When a client registers through Always Open, we pass the business’s legal name, entity type, EIN, address, website and the registering person’s name, title, email and phone to our texting provider (Twilio) and the carriers for that check. We do not keep the EIN once it has been sent.

After-hours AI line

If a client adds our after-hours AI line, the assistant tells every caller it is an AI and that the call may be recorded. Calls may be recorded and transcribed so we can pass the details and a summary to the business. Callers can ask to speak to the business directly.

The assistant may call a customer back for the business, only when the customer asked for a call back on that matter or agreed to a check-in call. Those calls say up front that they are automated and may be recorded. A customer can end them for good by pressing 9 or saying stop, and we keep that choice.

The Always Open app

Our clients use the Always Open app, on the web and on iPhone, to approve replies and posts, see calls and messages, change their AI line settings and add follow-ups. The app keeps the client’s private link on the device, plus the first name they enter so their monthly record shows who approved what. If a client turns on notifications, we keep that device’s notification token so we can send alerts. Photos a client sends for a post are deleted after 60 days. Names and phone numbers a client adds for a review request or a follow-up are used only for that request or reminder, and a review request goes only to a customer the client says agreed to it. The app has no ads and no tracking. To remove the app’s data from a phone, sign out in the app’s Settings or delete the app. To have us delete what we hold, email us.

Who we share it with

Only as needed to provide the service, and never for their own marketing:

  • Service providers we use to run Always Open, such as our texting and phone provider (Twilio), Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), website hosting, and payment or invoicing services.
  • The business you contacted. If you are a customer of one of our clients, your messages and details go to that client.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, and a buyer if Always Open is ever sold, with the same protections.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide the service and meet legal and accounting requirements. When a client leaves, we remove our access to their accounts promptly.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us.
  • Stop texts at any time by replying STOP.
  • If you are a client, you can remove our access to your Google and Facebook accounts yourself at any time.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including limiting access to the accounts and data we need. No method of storing or sending information is perfect, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

Our service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date. If a change is significant, we will tell our clients directly.
