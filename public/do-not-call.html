Do Not Call policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This is the written Do Not Call policy of Lake Country Foam Party, LLC, which does business as Always Open (“we”). It covers Outreach calls: short automated calls we place to businesses for our business clients, asking whether the called business wants to hear from our client. A copy is available to anyone on request.

Stop the calls. Say “stop” or press 9 on any call, call back the number that called you and ask to be taken off the list, use alwaysopenoffice.com/stop-calls, or email hello@alwaysopenoffice.com. The number is never called again by any business we place calls for.

Our do-not-call list

  • Every request not to be called goes on our do-not-call list right away, for every business we place calls for, not only the one that called.
  • We keep each request for at least five years. In practice we keep them for good.
  • Our list is checked before every call.

Checks before every call

  • The National Do Not Call Registry, state do-not-call lists and a list of people known to sue over calls, using a version of each no more than 31 days old. In practice each number is checked when it is added and again within a day before it is called.
  • The line itself: we call only landlines whose caller ID belongs to a business. Cell phones and home phones are never called.
  • Never hospitals, health care facilities, schools, police, fire or other emergency lines.
  • Never numbers in California, Florida, Indiana, Minnesota or Oklahoma.
  • Never more than one call to a number in 30 days for the same client, or in 7 days across all of them.

How calls are made

  • Weekdays from 9 AM to 5 PM where the called number is, and never on federal holidays.
  • Each call says at the start that it is an automated assistant, names the business it is calling for, and says how to stop the calls. It gives a number where that business can be reached, and the number that called you can be called back to ask not to be called.
  • No sales message is left on an answering machine.
  • Our clients sign Outreach terms before any call. They promise their lists are businesses they have the right to contact, and to pass along the same day any request not to be called that they receive directly. Lists are reviewed before calls go out.

Training and records

  • Everyone at Always Open who works on Outreach is trained on this policy before they do.
  • We keep a record of every call: when it was placed, what was said, the checks it passed and what happened.
