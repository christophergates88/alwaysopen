# Always Open: how we write

Every word on the website, in the app, in a notification or an email follows this. If a line doesn't, fix the line.

## The voice in one scene

> Bottom of the ninth. Your kid is up. A customer is calling. Max has it. Look up.

A moment you'd hate to miss. An interruption. "Max has it." A quiet instruction. That shape is the whole voice.

## Rules

1. **Straight face.** Short, plain sentences. Serious delivery. The joke is understatement and truth, never a wink.
2. **Lead with the moment, not the feature.** Two lenses, used in balance:
   - **Lens A, the owner gets the moment back:** the kids' game, dinner, bedtime, the roof at sunset, hole 14, Dad's seventieth.
   - **Lens B, the business is never closed, seen from the customer's side:** Sunday 11:12 PM, Christmas morning, 12:15 PM when everyone is on a job, 5:01 PM when the office just closed, a sick day, a storm warning at 2 AM.
   The feature name is the small label under the moment, not the headline.
3. **Say what owners already know about themselves.** "You could. You won't. Not because you're lazy." Never insult them, and never make fun of the customer.
4. **One concrete image per section.** The review from Tuesday. Under a house. 5:01 PM. Not three images, one.
5. **End on a quiet instruction or a dry line.** "Look up." "Go home." "We'll wait."
6. **Always honest.** No ranking promises. No invented numbers (samples are labeled as samples). "Coming soon" tags stay until the thing works. Say plainly what our team does by hand and what runs on its own.
7. **The comedy dial.** Home page, builders page and the app: full deadpan. Insurance, medical, legal and any regulated page: warm and calm, no jokes about claims, rates, coverage, patients or money. Legal pages, billing, errors, consent and Compliance Guard text: plain and clear, no jokes. Clarity beats a joke anywhere money, consent or an error is involved.
8. **Words we don't use.** "AI" appears once, in the hero line "Nobody likes talking to AI. Call ours anyway." Everywhere else Max is "your phone assistant" or "an automated assistant", and he never reads as a person. (The spoken call greeting and the privacy and terms pages keep the required disclosures; they are not marketing.) Never "local": the service is nationwide. Never a team member's first name: it's "our team".
9. **Max is never a person.** He says what he is up front on every call. Copy never implies otherwise.

## Before and after

| Feature-first | Moment-first |
|---|---|
| Missed-call text-back | You were under a sink. They still got an answer. *(Missed-call text-back, coming soon)* |
| Invoice reminders with a pay link | The invoice from the 20th is still open. The reminder is written. You tap OK. |
| New call from Linda | Max has it. Linda needs a quote. Keep going. |
| No requests yet | Quiet day. Enjoy it. |
| 3 items waiting | 3 things need your OK. Take a minute when you can. |
| Monthly report | One page a month. What moved, and what we did. |

## The FAQ test

Answer the question first, in one short sentence. Then the reason. Then one dry line, if it earns its place. If it doesn't, leave it out.
